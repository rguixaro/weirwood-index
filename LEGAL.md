## Hosted service

The hosted service can return bounded passage previews with a book, chapter, and point-of-view
reference. These previews are search results from a privately operated index. They are not part of
the Apache-licensed project code or documentation, and all rights in the source text remain with
the applicable rights holders.

The service uses result limits, rate limits, and bot verification to discourage bulk or sequential
extraction. It is a passage-location and reference tool, not a source for reading or reproducing
the books.
